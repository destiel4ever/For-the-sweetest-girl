Because you're amazing, Zai. You're beautiful, you're smart, and you're really funny

So today, I hope you let yourself be celebrated. Eat something you love, laugh a lot, take a million pictures, and enjoy being the gorgeous birthday girl you are. ♡

Thank you for being part of my life. I'm really, really glad I met you.

Happy birthday, Zai.
I love you so much. ♡
